Our Story

Started with a Truck and a Promise

In 2004, Jeff Callahan started with a single pickup truck, a crew of two, and a simple promise: treat every homeowner the way he'd want his own parents treated. No upselling. No disappearing after the check clears. Just honest work at a fair price.

Twenty years and over 2,400 roofs later, that promise hasn't changed. Jeff still personally reviews every estimate and walks every completed job before his crew calls it done.

We've grown into a team of 18 certified roofers, three project managers, and two dedicated insurance claim specialists — but we're still the same family business we've always been.
