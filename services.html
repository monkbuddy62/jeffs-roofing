Roof Replacement

A full roof replacement is the best long-term investment you can make in your home. We tear off the old roofing system down to the deck, inspect for rot and damage, and install a brand new system built to last 25–50 years.

We are GAF Master Elite® certified contractors, which allows us to offer the industry-leading Golden Pledge® warranty — one of the best protections available in roofing.

  • Architectural, 3-tab, and designer shingles
  • Metal roofing (standing seam, corrugated)
  • Tile and slate roofing
  • Full deck inspection and repair
  • Ice and water shield installation
  • Manufacturer warranties up to 50 years

We're on your side — not the insurance company's. Our goal is to get your roof replaced at no out-of-pocket cost to you.

Leak Detection & Repair

Roof leaks are tricky — where water enters the attic is often not where it appears on your ceiling. Our technicians use moisture meters and thermal imaging to locate the true source of every leak.

We don't just patch symptoms. We fix the root cause, then verify the repair holds before we leave your property.

  • Same-day emergency response available
  • Flashing repair and replacement
  • Valley and ridge cap repairs
  • Chimney and skylight sealing
  • Attic moisture assessment
  • Written warranty on all repairs
